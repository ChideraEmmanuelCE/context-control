03 / DESIGN INTENT

Three principles behind the concept.

01 — OBSERVE

Bring context together.

Show the relevant signals from connected business systems in one reviewable place, with source and timing visible.

02 — EXPLAIN

Make uncertainty legible.

Surface what conflicts, what is missing, and why confidence is insufficient to proceed automatically.

03 — GOVERN

Keep the human in control.

Hold consequential actions until an authorized person reviews the evidence and decides what happens next.
